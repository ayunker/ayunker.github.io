I enjoy reading monthly updates from [people][arne] [I][macwright]
[follow][duarte] (via RSS), they're always so interesting. So I figured I'd try
writing my own. It'll be good to 1) get me into a regular writing cadence (I
want to write more, here and elsewhere, this year), and 2) maybe someone will
find mine interesting (jk I don't think people read this blog). Anyways, let's
give this a try!

## general

Greetings from 30,000 feet[^1]. This month's edition is a little delayed, and
I'm writing this on the flight home from Prague (more on that in next month's
edition!).

September was a big month. After 4 incredible years at Hashrocket, it was time
to move on. I started a new job in the latter half of the month.

I had a glorious week off in between. It was a mostly relaxing week - I spent a
lot of time reading, writing, kitchenning, setting up a computer, and hanging
out at Great Lakes.

There was also a non-relaxing portion, where I went for a _long_ bike ride (at
least for me). 50 miles along the lakefront. Down to Jackson Harbor, all the
way to the North end of the trail, then back down to Promontory Point before
the final stretch back up home. It was a beast of a ride. Luckily I picked a
lovely day for it, but I was dying at the end nonetheless. I'm super proud I
managed it, though.

## using

I picked up a [Uni Zento Flow][zento] at Atlas Stationers. I was on the fence
about the Zento before I purchased it, and I think I still am. On the one hand
it's super smooth, super nice to write with. On the other hand, I don't know if
it's really any better than a Zebra Sarasa or other similar gel pen. Though the
Zento is a liquid gel/rollerball-y type pen, it really just feels like a gel
pen, and writes like one too. What convinced me to pull the trigger on it was
the metal barrel of the Flow model. I like the added heft, and even if I'm not
enamored with the refill I can swap it out for something I prefer more. It's a
nice pen to write with.

I'm considering going scorched earth on my [neovim config][nvim_config]. As I
set up my new work machine, I ran into a number of things that didn't work out
of the box. Things like commenting lines of code, standardrb integration with
the lsp. I could try to fix them, but it's frustrating. It's got me thinking of
trying helix or recreating a very pared down nvim config. Realistically the
pared down config is more likely, and I think I could get it pretty lean. Helix
is intriguing but would require some muscle memory relearning. Something to
experiment with.

## reading

I finished _The Golden Notebook_. It was incredibly interesting, but a very
challenging read. It took me most of the month to work through, though I felt
like I was reading at a good clip (I guess that means it was a long book).

I also read _The Unknown_, by Riley Sager. It was...ok. Interesting premise,
but I think too many twists and a less than satisfying conclusion.

I have like 3 books going right now, with 2 close to finished. I need to wrap
those up and get back to 1 book in progress at a time - any more than that just
gives me anxiety.

## watching

We watched _Gone_ on Britbox. It was very good, very intense. Proper drama.
Makes Bristol look (hauntingly) beautiful. I'd move there, but I don't think
I'd like that bridge.

We're working on the _Marble Hall Murders_ and _Marlow Murder Club_. Both solid,
cosy, and familiar.

Taskmaster Series 22 has started, but it's only ok. It has it's moments for
sure, but one contestant will be the winner by quite a margin, so the
competition is a little one-sided.

## listening

I went to my first show this year (crazy to think that it's September) - I saw
Alcest and Deafheaven open for Mastodon. I could care less about Mastodon, but
Alcest and Deafheaven is a dream lineup of blackened shoegaze. Alcest was
sublime as always, and Deafheaven ripped. They debuted a new song - _Hunter_ -
and it's the heaviest thing they've done to date. Proper black metal. So
excited for the EP.

Leading up to the show, I was binging lots of Alcest and Deafheaven, natch.
Still getting set up on the work laptop and I don't have my Godspeed on there
yet, so I've been relying on youtube for their works. But an ad break in the
middle of _Sleep_ is really jarring, so I need to get those files setup pronto.
